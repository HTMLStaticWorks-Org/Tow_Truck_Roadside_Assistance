Driver Safety

While you wait & what helps dispatch

Roadside Safety First

  • Turn on hazard flashers and pull as far right as possible
  • Stay buckled inside the vehicle if on a high-speed highway
  • Exit only from the passenger side away from traffic lanes
  • Keep your phone ringer on for operator arrival calls

Helpful Info to Include

  • Exact highway direction, exit number, or cross street
  • Vehicle make, drivetrain (AWD/EV), and tire condition
  • Parking garage height clearance or gate code if applicable
  • Preferred destination repair shop or home address

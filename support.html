FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about arrival times, upfront pricing, EV towing, and insurance claims.

How fast can a tow truck reach my location?

Our median metro arrival time is 22 minutes. Because TowGuard stages patrol trucks across 18 regional zones, we route the closest unit directly via live GPS telemetry.

Do you charge extra for night, weekend, or rain dispatches?

No surge pricing ever. Your upfront quote shown in our cost estimator and dispatch portal applies 24/7, including nights, holidays, and severe weather.

Can you safely tow all-wheel drive and electric vehicles?

Yes. We exclusively dispatch low-angle hydraulic flatbeds with nylon wheel-net straps and skate dollies for EVs and AWD vehicles to eliminate drivetrain strain.

How do I track my assigned tow truck?

Once your request is confirmed, you receive an SMS link and access inside your Dispatch Portal showing the truck's live map position, operator name, and countdown ETA.

Will my auto insurance reimburse a TowGuard tow?

Yes. Every completed dispatch automatically generates an itemized PDF invoice and pre/post-tow inspection log formatted for instant carrier reimbursement or direct partner billing.

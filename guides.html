First 5 Minutes Protocol

What to Do Before Help Arrives

Follow these four commander-approved roadside steps the moment your vehicle loses power or suffers a blowout.

01
Step 01 • Immediate Action

Signal Early & Coast to a Flat Right Shoulder

Keep both hands at 9 and 3 o'clock, avoid slamming the brakes, and glide smoothly past narrow overpasses or blind curves onto firm ground.

02
Step 02 • Scene Visibility

Activate Hazards & Angle Wheels Away From Traffic

Turn on hazard flashers right away, turn your front wheels toward the guardrail edge, and switch on interior dome lights at dusk or in heavy rain.

03
Step 03 • Priority Dispatch

Drop Your GPS Pin & Note Drivetrain Clearance

Submit your exact GPS pin, AWD/EV status, or parking-garage height limit on TowGuard so dispatch sends the exact flatbed or compact rig on the first roll.

04
Step 04 • Stay Protected

Wait Buckled Inside or Behind the Guardrail

Never stand behind your bumper or walk along active expressway lanes. Exit only from the non-traffic passenger door if stepping off the roadway is safer.

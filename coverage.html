Coverage Guidelines

Standard vs. Heavy Dispatch

Covered in Standard Zones

  • All passenger cars, crossovers, SUVs & pickup trucks under 10,000 lbs
  • EV & AWD flatbed transport with wheel skates included
  • Low-clearance parking decks down to 6 ft 2 in height
  • Up to 100 miles per tow with TowGuard Shield plans

Requires Heavy Specialist

  • Class 7–8 semi-tractors and loaded 53-ft trailers
  • Over-water crane lifts or hazardous cargo spills
  • Unmaintained backcountry trails without truck access
  • Multi-state cross-country carrier relocations

FAQ

Operator questions

What to expect from the moment your TowGuard technician accepts your call.

Are TowGuard operators trained for electric and AWD vehicles?

Yes. Every TowGuard flatbed operator completes EV high-voltage isolation checks, transport-mode engagement, and zero-drag wheel-skate loading before taking solo dispatches.

Can I ride in the tow truck cab with my operator?

Yes. Our crew-cab flatbeds seat up to two passengers comfortably with climate control, or you can arrange a contactless key handoff and track delivery live from your phone.

Do I need to tip or pay extra cash on scene?

Never. Your quoted rate or Shield membership covers the complete dispatch digitally, and your itemized receipt is emailed and saved to your Dispatch history automatically.
